Foreword ✦

An interactive workbook for the third term of Grade 11 General — drawing together campus life, online education, academic integrity, student housing, and city & global issues into a single practice journal for grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening and speaking.

Open a section from the chapter strip above, or hit Contents for the full table. If your teacher has shared a class link, type your name in Student, work through the practice, and submit your progress when you're done.

A Note on Method
Read each prompt twice. Choose the option you can explain. Treat every wrong answer as a note to yourself.
