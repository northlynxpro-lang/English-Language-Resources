Foreword ✦

An interactive workbook for the third term of Grade 11 Advanced — drawing together A Universal Language? and Each to their Own into a single practice journal for grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening and speaking.

Open a section from the chapter strip above, or hit Contents for the full table. If your teacher has shared a class link, type your name in Student, work through the practice, and submit your progress when you're done.

A Note on Method
Read the prompt twice. Choose the option you can defend with evidence. Treat every wrong answer as a note to yourself.

Grammar Focus

Reviewed across the term's exercises.

  • Phrasal verbs — separable & non-separable
  • Adverbial linkers for cohesion
  • Reported speech & reporting verbs
  • Subordinating conjunctions
  • The past perfect, active & passive
  • Present modals & semi-modals
  • Past simple & used to
  • The past perfect continuous
  • Verb patterns
  • The third conditional
Vocabulary Themes

From Animal Communication to Generation Z.

  • Animal communication
  • Body language
  • Language development
  • Cyberbullying
  • Likes & dislikes
  • The Silk Road
  • Architecture
  • UAE landmarks
  • Generation Z
  • Intercultural understanding
